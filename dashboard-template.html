Quick Start Guide
Whitespace & Cross-Sell Opportunity Analyzer — Account Executives & Sales Management
↓ Download User Guide
Three common starting points — pick the one that matches your role.
AE
Weekly Territory Review
Account Executives — start here every week
Start on: Overview page
  1. Filter to your Sales Group using the filter bar at the top
  2. Check the KPI tiles — note Total Whitespace Gap and High-Priority Accounts
  3. Go to the Account Details table → sort by Whitespace Gap descending
  4. Click into your top accounts → review whitespace Solution Areas, Gap to Wallet, and Recommended Solutions
  5. Use the scope toggle (Planning Entity / Planning Entity + Top Parent) to factor in parent-company coverage before your call
Pro tip: Use the account search (top right of the filter bar) to jump directly to any account by name — no scrolling required.
Mgr
QBR / Business Review Prep
Sales Managers — before a review or QBR
Start on: Overview page
  1. Review the Treemap — largest boxes show the biggest dollar gaps in your territory
  2. Switch to Whitespace Accounts mode to compare by account count instead of dollar gap
  3. Scan the Heatmap by Sales Group — palest cells = most accounts missing that solution
  4. Open Account Detail for the top accounts per Solution Area to prep coaching conversations
  5. Go to Cluster Analysis to find shared plays across the team — look for large clusters with high priority signals
Pro tip: If High-Priority Accounts is large relative to Accounts with Whitespace, you have active buying signals across the team — prioritize those accounts in your next 1:1s.
PL
Campaign Planning
Portfolio & Industry Leads — build focused plays
Start on: Cluster Analysis
  1. Open Cluster Analysis — clusters are ranked by total Gap to Wallet
  2. Filter by Solution Area to narrow to a specific play
  3. Scan cluster cards for large account counts, big gap, and Very High / High priority — all visible at a glance on each card
  4. Click a cluster → check Target Products for the shared whitespace opportunity
  5. Coordinate cross-AE outreach on the same play to multiply impact
Pro tip: If 40 accounts share Finance + HCM coverage but are missing CX and SCM — that's one campaign for all 40. Cluster Analysis surfaces exactly these patterns.
Glossary
Term What It Means
Whitespace A Solution Area the account doesn't own yet — not live, not in project. This is the cross-sell gap.
Gap to Wallet Uncaptured budget estimate — based on what similar companies in the same industry and revenue tier actually spend. Bigger gap = more money still on the table.
TAO Priority AI-scored likelihood that an opportunity will open in the next 12 months. Scale: 1 Very High → 5 Low. Priorities 1 and 2 are the primary action tier — they represent the top 5% of accounts and ~40% of future opportunities.
TAO Strategy Tells you how to approach the account based on wallet share and priority: Attack (low share, high priority), Expand (high share, high priority), Digitally Nurture (low share, low priority), Defend (high share, low priority).
Shelfware Account has the license but isn't using it. Shows as covered, not whitespace — this is a re-adoption conversation, not a new sale.
RISE Customer Account already on RISE with SAP. Often the fastest path to expansion — they're already committed to the SAP ecosystem.
Planning Entity The subsidiary-level account in SAP's CRM — the unit all data is attached to. Always check whether you're looking at a subsidiary vs. the parent.
Top Parent The ultimate parent company. May already own solutions shown as whitespace at the Planning Entity level — use "Planning Entity + Top Parent" scope mode to account for this.
